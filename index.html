QuikSnap
for iPhone

Job-site photos, straight into the right Google Drive folder.

Crews take hundreds of photos a week, and they end up scattered across phones, texts and emails. QuikSnap fixes that: pick the project, tap the shutter, and the photo is in that project's Drive folder before you've put the phone away.

Coming soon to the App Store

What it does

One tap, filed

Every photo uploads to the project folder you chose, named Name_Date_Time.jpg so the office knows who took it and when.

Works offline

No signal on site? Photos queue on the phone and upload the moment you're back online.

2-in-1 and 3-in-1

Stitch two or three shots side by side into one image — before and after, wide and close-up.

Your Drive, your data

QuikSnap has no server. Photos go only from your phone to your own Google Drive.

How it works

  1. Sign in with Google and allow access to Google Drive.
  2. Choose the Drive folder your team uses for projects. Each subfolder is a project; your office can share one folder with the whole crew.
  3. Pick a project, tap Start Shooting, and take photos. Turn on "New folder for each date" to get a dated subfolder per day.

Google account access

QuikSnap requests access to Google Drive so it can list your folders, create project folders, and upload photos. It never reads or changes files other than the folders and photos it manages for you. You can remove access at any time from Settings → Sign out, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Details are in the privacy policy.
